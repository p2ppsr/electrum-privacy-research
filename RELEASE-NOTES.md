# Research release 0.1.0

This release contains the claim-to-evidence ledger, plain-English shopkeeper explainer, diagrams, versioned source and infrastructure observations, official macOS wallet capture, valid-chain shop/departure demonstration, certificate matrix, routing tests and reproduction tools. The underlying observations were collected October 28–29, 2026.

Independent review, replications, corrections and additional evidence are invited through the repository's Issues and pull requests. [Open questions](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md) and [contribution guidance](CONTRIBUTING.md) replace the earlier private-review process. Advance independent review, direct maintainer outreach, right-of-reply arrangements and reader studies are not release conditions. No direct outreach is planned as part of this release.

The evidence retains its scientific limits: ElectrumR's matching source/build was unavailable; the valid-chain adapter is a documented research component; no real-chain money or real merchant was involved; observations of interests do not prove legal ownership; independent human replication and ordinary-user comprehension have not been measured. These are boundaries on the claims, not publication blockers. Future results can refine the record through open review.

The existing `private-prepublication-20261029-rc1` tag and its archived preparation receipt preserve an earlier historical snapshot. Review prerequisites recorded in that receipt were superseded by the open-review policy above. Those old records and failed experimental samples remain intact for provenance. Current entry points are [README](README.md), [CLAIMS](CLAIMS.md) and [OPEN-QUESTIONS](OPEN-QUESTIONS.md).
